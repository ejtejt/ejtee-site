About the Artist

Creative designer and lettering artist

Stroke by stroke

EJ is a Singapore-based designer with a background in graphic and product design. Today, he creates bespoke lettering, illustration, and sign-painted work for corporate and private clients across a range of mediums.

He also shares his craft through hands-on workshops, helping more people explore lettering and develop confidence in their own creative practice.

Creating a richer visual landscape, stroke by stroke.
